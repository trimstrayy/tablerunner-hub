Typography & page layout guidance (use when converting to slides or PDF):

- Recommended page size: A4 landscape (297mm × 210mm) or 11" × 8.5" (Letter landscape).
- Title font: Inter Black / Roboto Slab Bold, 44–56 pt for slide title.
- Body font: Inter / Roboto, 16–20 pt for body text, 22–28 pt for subheads.
- Margins: 24 mm (0.95 in) all around. Keep safe area inside these margins.
- Line height: 1.3–1.5 for body copy. Use left alignment for paragraphs.
- Export: SVG for diagrams, PNG at 300 DPI for raster export, PDF for final deck.

Notes: a plain-text slide deck cannot enforce fonts — use the typography guidance above when you import into PowerPoint/Google Slides/Keynote or when generating PDFs.
